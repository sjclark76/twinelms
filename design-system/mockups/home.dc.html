About us

We’re Jo and Stuart

We moved to Lake Hawea with our children Jessiah and Zoe in 2021. We’re passionate about the outdoors and love to run, ski, mountain bike, hike and ride horses. We love sharing our slice of paradise with guests and we hope you love it too.
