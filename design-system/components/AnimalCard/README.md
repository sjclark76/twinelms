# AnimalCard

A card that introduces one of the family's animals, the warmest moment on the site. The consumer provides a round photo (64px, in `nest-animal-face`; the tussock circle with an initial stands in until photos exist), the name in display type, the breed in slate and one or two friendly sentences.

Always use the animals' names. The chickens (Duchess, Zelda, Thelma and Louise) share one card.
