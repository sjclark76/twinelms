**Our Animals**

**Dexter the dog**

Dexter is a New Zealand Huntaway – a quintessential New Zealand farm dog commonly found riding on the back of quad bikes across rugged farmland. Huntaway’s were bred for moving sheep and cows by using their big loud bark and are known for being very friendly and smart. Dexter is afraid of stock animals and loud machinery so became a much loved part of our family.  He loves to meet people, and will play all day – and can become ‘talkative’ when excited. Please let us know if you do not like dogs and we will ensure that he is put away.
